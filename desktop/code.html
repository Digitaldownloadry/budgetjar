The Student Choice Simulator

Practice realistic financial trade-offs without risking real money. Experience the ripple effect on your goals in real time.

psychology_alt Scenario #04 • Mid-Month Dilemma

“It’s the 22nd of the month. You have ₹3,500 unallocated money left in your cushion. Your hostel friends are planning a weekend cafe dinner (₹1,400), but your mid-term project needs a ₹1,200 reference book.”

Option A groups

Go all-in on dinner with friends

Spend ₹1,400 on dinner, borrow the project book from the university library reserve shelf for 2-hour increments.

Laptop goal pushed back 18 days
Option B • Balanced Recommended

Split the difference

Join for coffee & dessert (₹400) + buy the reference book (₹1,200) + stash ₹1,900 into your laptop jar.

Maintains ₹410/day safe runway
Option C lock

Monk Mode Discipline

Skip dinner entirely, purchase the book (₹1,200), and stash all remaining ₹2,300 straight into savings.

Accelerates laptop goal by 12 days
Impact Analysis • Option B Selected
Sustainable Balance

You protected social connection by showing up for dessert without bearing a ₹1,400 bill. You secured your academic essential, and still grew your savings jar by ₹1,900. Your daily safe burn remains a comfortable ₹395/day through the 30th.
